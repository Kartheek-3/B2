Prompt-5: CH02 Flexible Doctor Availability Strategy Implementation
================================================================================
Implement Change 2 under Part2/changes/Change2/:
- Strategy Interface: IAvailabilityStrategy (validateSlot, generateAvailableSlots).
- Concrete Strategies:
  1. StandardBusinessHoursStrategy: Mon-Fri 09:00-17:00, lunch break 12:00-13:00, weekend rejection, conflict detection.
  2. ShiftBasedAvailabilityStrategy: Rotating shifts (morning/evening/night), assigned shift day validation, shift boundary enforcement.
  3. EmergencyOnCallAvailabilityStrategy: 24/7 emergency triage availability with mandatory 15-minute recovery buffer gap around bookings.
- Context: DoctorAvailabilityContext with dynamic runtime strategy switching and server-side validation.
- Automated Test Suite: test_strategy.ts.

================================================================================
Prompt-6: CH03 Automated Reminders Observer Pattern Implementation
================================================================================
Implement Change 3 under Part2/changes/Change3/:
- Subject: DomainEventBus with subscribe, unsubscribe, and publish.
- Domain Events: APPOINTMENT_SCHEDULED, APPOINTMENT_CANCELLED, APPOINTMENT_RESCHEDULED.
- Concrete Observers:
  1. ReminderSchedulerObserver: Computes T-24h and T-2h reminder windows; enqueues tasks with deterministic idempotency keys.
  2. ReminderCancellationObserver: Automatically invalidates/revokes pending reminders on cancellation.
  3. AuditLogObserver: Records chronological audit trail.
- Persistent Store: JsonFileReminderStore with disk persistence and duplicate prevention.
- Processing Engine: ReminderProcessingEngine evaluating due tasks, transitioning states, retrying transient errors.
- Automated Test Suite: test_observer.ts.
